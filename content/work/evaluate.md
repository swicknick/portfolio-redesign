## The problem
Trading NFTs person to person was confusing and risky. Evaluate let people trade **across multiple collections** on the Flow blockchain, something new that people had to understand before they'd trust it.

That meant three jobs: explain the idea (website), make trading feel safe and simple (product), and teach new users the multi-step trade (onboarding).

## My role
UX/UI and motion designer on the Evaluate team at Variable Medium, working across the website, the trading product (desktop and mobile), and the onboarding video.

## The product: trade + chat
- **Trade table:** a system for selecting and swapping NFTs from both sides of a deal.
- **Chat built into the trade:** negotiation happens where the trade happens, not in a separate app.
- **Offers that expire:** traders don't have to be online at the same time. Offers wait, with an expiry date, so trading works asynchronously.
- **Mobile:** the app was redesigned for small screens, keeping every key feature and the visual style while resizing for legibility.
- **Tested with users:** advanced mobile prototypes let us test layout and flows with users before development.
