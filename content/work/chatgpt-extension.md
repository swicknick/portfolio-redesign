## The problem
Switching to a separate ChatGPT window breaks focus. Busy professionals, students, and researchers lose their place every time they leave the page they're working on.

## The idea
Bring ChatGPT into Chrome itself, so help shows up next to the work instead of in place of it.

## Design decisions
- **Feels native to both products:** visual language drawn from ChatGPT and Chrome, so it reads as part of the browser, not a pop-up.
- **Motion that explains space:** custom easing and isometric views show where the assistant lives relative to the page, so opening it feels like expanding the workspace rather than covering it.
