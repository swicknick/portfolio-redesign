## The problem
Shep (formerly Edisoft) simplifies supply-chain complexity, but the product is abstract and technical. After a rebrand, the site needed to make it click fast.

## What I built
One consistent visual language across 10 pieces:
- **Hero:** the "Shep Fabric"
- **"Spaghetti to Blueprint":** a transformation from tangled systems to a clear one
- **Five capability animations:** Automate, Protect, See Everything, Scale, Launch Fast
- **Three architecture illustrations:** One Language, Decoupled Systems, Centralized Logic & Observability

## Performance
The hero first shipped on Rive's WebGL2 renderer, and the page scored 40 on Lighthouse. Switching it to the Canvas runtime brought the score to **99**, with the same animation.

## Why it matters
Explaining how a system works is information design. Every piece turns an abstract idea (automation, security, scale) into something a buyer understands in seconds.
