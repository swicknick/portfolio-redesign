Nicholas wants a **full blitz**: every channel, as fast as possible. Read `plan.md`, `career/applications.md`, and the resume source `career/resume/build.py` first.

## Ground rules
1. **Confidential.** Nicholas still works at Webstacks.
   - Never turn on LinkedIn "Open to Work," not even recruiter-only. He decided against it.
   - Before any LinkedIn profile edit, turn **off** "Share profile updates with your network."
   - Never message anyone connected to Webstacks.
2. **Approval in batches, then go.**
   - Show Nicholas a batch: role, company, link, pay, and why it fits.
   - Once he approves the batch, submit all of those applications without stopping again for each one.
   - Never send a LinkedIn message, a connection note, or an agency profile without approval of the text.
3. **Pay filter:**
   - Full-time: at least $80k, target $100k+. (Floor lowered from $85k on 2026-10-08 to widen the pool.)
   - 1099 contract: at least $55/hr, target $70+.
   - Agency W2 contract: at least $50/hr.
   - Never enter his current rate. If a form insists on it, put the target instead.
4. **Location:** fully remote US, or hybrid/on-site in the **Vancouver, WA / Portland** area. **Never Seattle.**
5. **Titles to search:**
   - Motion Designer
   - Senior Motion Designer
   - Product Motion Designer
   - UX Motion Designer
   - Technical Motion Designer
   - Motion Engineer
   - Interactive Designer
   - Creative Technologist
   - Rive Animator
   - Brand/Motion Designer at SaaS or AI companies
6. **Skip:**
   - heavy-3D roles (Houdini, 10+ years)
   - pure video editing or social-ad mills
   - roles posted more than about 3 months ago (relaxed from 30 days on 2026-10-07), or reposted again and again
7. **Log everything** in `career/applications.md`: date, company, role, link, pay, status. Commit after each batch.
